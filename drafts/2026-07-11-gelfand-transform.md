title: The Gelfand Transform
category: Maths
---

Let $$A$$ be a C*-algebra, and consider the set of characters 

$$ \Omega(A) = \{ \chi : A \to \mathbb{C} : \chi\; \text{is a homomorphism} \}. $$

Then given an element $$a \in A$$, we define the Gelfand transform of $$a$$ as the element
$$\hat{a} : \Omega(A) \to \mathbb{C}$$ by

$$ \hat{a}(\chi) = \chi(a). $$
